## Research Questions
- **RQ1:** How can Android-specific contextual features be systematically leveraged to uncover access control vulnerabilities that are missed by existing techniques?
- **RQ2:** How can these methods be adapted to detect similar flaws in Android-based platforms such as Android Automotive by accounting for platform-specific architectural differences?

## Contributions (examples)
1. **Ariadne:** A technique to detect access-control inconsistencies in data-driven Android customizations via an AC dependency-graph abstraction.
2. **RepFinder:** A static analysis pipeline to identify access-control anomalies caused by code-cloned (replicated) APIs in customized Android frameworks across vendors.
3. **AutoAcRaptor:** A pipeline for uncovering access-control and feature-check gaps specific to Android Automotive OS (AAOS).
